How to format the CSV

One row per case. A deidID column, then one column per feature coded 1 (present) or 0 (absent).
ColumnFeaturePoints
  • Column names aren't case-sensitive, and spaces or underscores in them are ignored. Column order doesn't matter.
  • The download is your file with four columns added: pbi_score (raw), pbi_score_rescaled (raw ÷ 81 × 100), pbi_band and pbi_flag. Any other columns (age, GCS, outcome) are kept as they are.
  • Every feature cell needs a 1 or 0. A blank cell is not read as 0: the row is flagged and left unscored, so missing data can't lower a score silently.
  • Use de-identified IDs only.

Weights are normalized so the full feature set sums to 100. Because trajectory level and
       ventricular blood extent are each graded as one choice, the highest attainable total is 81.
       The rescaled value (raw ÷ 81 × 100) is a readability aid; the weights, bands and
       thresholds are unchanged and apply to the raw score.

  


  

    Intended use
    
Research and educational tool. Not a substitute for clinical judgment.
       The score was derived retrospectively at a single Level 1 trauma center and has not been
       externally validated. It is specific to penetrating injury and does not apply to blunt TBI.

    
Mortality figures reported below are observed frequencies in the published cohort, not
       individual predicted probabilities. Outcomes in that cohort were influenced by withdrawal
       of life-sustaining therapy. No patient data entered here leaves this page.
