Intended use

Research and educational tool. Not a substitute for clinical judgment. The score was derived retrospectively at a single Level 1 trauma center and has not been externally validated. It is specific to penetrating injury and does not apply to blunt TBI.

Mortality figures reported below are observed frequencies in the published cohort, not individual predicted probabilities. Outcomes in that cohort were influenced by withdrawal of life-sustaining therapy. No patient data entered here leaves this page.
